Privacy Policy for Neon-Rush

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Neon-Rush respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use the Neon-Rush mobile game.

Information We Collect

Neon-Rush does not require you to create an account with the developer and does not directly request personal information such as your name, email address, or physical address.

The app uses third-party services that may automatically collect or process certain information, such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising data, app interactions, diagnostic information, and performance data.

Advertising

Neon-Rush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information to provide, measure, and improve advertising. Depending on your location and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where required, Neon-Rush asks for your consent before certain advertising data is processed. You may be able to review or change your privacy choices from within the app.

Game Center

Neon-Rush uses Apple Game Center for gaming features such as leaderboards. When you use Game Center, information associated with your Game Center account and gameplay may be processed by Apple according to Apple's privacy policies.

Local Game Data

Game progress and settings, such as your high score, experience, unlocked content, credits, missions, and preferences, may be stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

Neon-Rush uses services provided by:

  • Google AdMob – advertising and related measurement
  • Apple Game Center – gaming services and leaderboards

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Neon-Rush is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and privacy choices may be handled according to applicable laws, platform requirements, and the policies of the third-party services used by the app.

Your Privacy Choices

Where applicable, you may be given options regarding the processing of data for advertising. Privacy and consent choices can be changed where such functionality is provided within the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, its features, or applicable privacy requirements change. The latest version will be available on this page.
